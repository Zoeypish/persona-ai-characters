Content Policy

Persona is a home for virtual, AI-made characters. These rules keep it safe for the people who visit, fair to artists and brands, and respectful of real people.

Last updated 28 November 2026

What you can post

  • Images of an original virtual character that you created, or that you have permission to run.
  • Images you made yourself with AI tools, or that you have the rights to use.
  • Outfits, places and products, as long as the image doesn't pretend a brand endorses your character when it doesn't.

Before every upload, creators confirm: "I own or have the rights to these images, and they don't show a real person without their consent." That confirmation is saved with each image.

What you can't post

  • Real people without consent. See the next section.
  • Other people's work. Characters, artwork, photos or logos you don't own or have permission to use.
  • Sexual content or nudity.
  • Anything that sexualises minors, or characters that look like children shown in a sexual or suggestive way. We remove it immediately, close the account and report it to the authorities.
  • Hate or harassment aimed at anyone because of who they are.
  • Graphic violence, or anything that encourages self-harm.
  • Scams, spam or illegal goods, including fake giveaways and misleading shop links.
  • Fake "real" photos. Don't present an AI image as a real photo of a real event, and don't remove or hide Persona's AI label.

Real people

A Persona character must not look like, be named after, or pretend to be a real person, including celebrities and influencers, unless that person has given you clear written consent. This covers lookalikes, face swaps and "deepfakes".

If you see your own face or likeness on Persona without your consent, send a takedown request. You don't need an account. We hide the image while we check.

AI labels and origin records

Every character on Persona is virtual, and every image carries an AI label. Open any image to see its origin record:

  • Made by: the creator or studio behind the character.
  • AI tool: the tool or model used, as given by the creator.
  • Added: when it was posted on Persona.
  • Prompt or recipe: if the creator chose to share it.
  • Source and rights: where the image comes from and the creator's rights confirmation.

Reports and takedowns

  • Report button. Every image has a "Report this image" link. Reports are private. Signed-in people can report each image once.
  • Hidden after 3 reports. When 3 different people report the same image, it's hidden from everyone until we review it.
  • Takedown requests. Rights owners and people shown in an image can use the takedown form without an account.
  • Our review. We look at every report and either restore the image or take it down for good. We aim to review reports within a few days, and faster for real-person and child-safety reports.

What happens if you break a rule

  • The image is taken down. The creator sees "Removed by Persona" in their Studio.
  • Repeated or serious problems can mean losing upload access or the account being closed.
  • If you think we got it wrong, use the takedown form and choose "Appeal a decision".
