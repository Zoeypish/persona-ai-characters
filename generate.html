Generate with AI

Use your own OpenAI or Claude account to design a character from one line, make its images, and publish it to Persona without leaving the Studio.

1. Your AI key

Your key goes straight from this browser to OpenAI or Anthropic. Persona never receives or stores it. Usage is billed to your own AI account (a character and three images usually cost well under $1).

Where do I get a key? · Models

OpenAI: platform.openai.com/api-keys. Claude: console.anthropic.com. A ChatGPT or Claude subscription is separate from API credit.
