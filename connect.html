Creator Studio

Connect AI

Plug Persona into Claude, ChatGPT or any AI app that supports connectors. Your assistant can then design characters with you and publish them, images and all, straight to your Studio.

1. Your API key

A key lets an AI tool act for your account: create characters and add images, within your upload allowance. Treat it like a password. You can remove a key at any time.

2. Your connector address

This is the address you give your AI app. It includes your key, so keep it private like a password.

Create a key above and the address fills in with it.

3. Add it to your AI app

Persona speaks MCP, the open standard AI apps use for connectors. Pick your app:

  1. Open connectors

    On claude.ai or the Claude app, go to Settings → Connectors and choose Add custom connector.

  2. Paste the address

    Name it Persona, paste your connector address from step 2, and press Add.

  3. Try it

    In a new chat, open the + menu, make sure Persona is switched on, and say "Design a new Persona character with me".

  1. Switch on developer mode

    In ChatGPT, go to Settings → Apps & Connectors → Advanced settings and turn on Developer mode. Needs a Plus, Pro, Business or Enterprise plan.

  2. Create the connector

    Back in Apps & Connectors, choose Create. Name it Persona, paste your connector address, set Authentication to No authentication (the key is already in the address), tick that you trust it, and press Create.

  3. Try it

    In a new chat, choose Developer mode from the + menu, switch on Persona, and ask it to design a character. ChatGPT can make the images too, and Persona saves them.
