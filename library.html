Where these come from

Each look was studied from a public Pinterest pin with lots of saves. The photo stays on Pinterest and is shown with Pinterest's own embed, with a link back to the pin. What we store is our own written description of the outfit, scene, camera, light and mood. Faces are invented: the face block never describes the person in the photo.
